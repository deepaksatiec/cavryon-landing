The problem

Financial data is everywhere. The decision has no home.

Important decisions depend on information distributed across accounting systems, bank accounts, payroll platforms, operational tools, contracts, and spreadsheets.

Teams assemble the facts manually, discuss the decision through email or meetings, and preserve only the final action. When assumptions change—or the decision returns—the work starts again.
